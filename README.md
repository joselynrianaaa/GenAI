# 🎨🧠 Generative AI Lab Activities

Welcome to my Generative AI Labs repository! This repo contains a series of hands-on assignments, experiments, and projects done as part of my **Generative AI Laboratory** course during my undergraduate Computer Science program.

---

## 📚 Course Overview

This lab is designed to provide practical exposure to the foundations and advanced techniques of Generative AI. Through a blend of code, theory, and experimentation, I’ve explored various generative models including **GANs**, **VAEs**, **Transformers**, and **Diffusion models**.
